## Date and calculator

### Can I set a previous date instead of today's date?
**Yes.** The calculator interface includes manually editable **day, month and year** fields and a separate **Use Today** shortcut. This is a core feature.

### Can I type a crane serial that starts with zeros?
The serial field preserves leading zeros.

### Are there different result levels?
The illustrated results interface displays **Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3** separately.

### Does it work with every Liebherr crane or LICCON generation?
Compatibility has not been established for every model, controller or software version. Consult the relevant manufacturer specifications and do not promise universal coverage.

## Trials, license sales and support

### Can I try it before buying?
The app has a built-in **trial process for eligible customers and devices**. Trial approval is determined by the app's existing server checks.

### Where can I buy or renew a license?
In the Client Portal, choose **Renew / Buy License**. For prices and license questions, use the **Support & Contact** section inside the app.

### Is there a separate licensing website?
No. Customer licensing and support inquiries are managed inside the Android application.

### What if my device changes?
Contact support and use the app's device-license management or transfer process where eligible. The backend controls eligibility.
