# Welcome to the LIE_CRANE V2 Community 👋

This is the proposed text for a **GitHub Discussions welcome post**. It can be pasted into the Discussions interface by a repository moderator. Publication or pinning of an actual Discussion must be confirmed separately.

**Welcome!** LIE_CRANE V2 is an independent Android engineering tool created for appropriately authorized mobile-crane technicians, maintenance engineers and technical support teams.

We'd love to hear from you:

- What would make the **manual day/month/year selection** workflow easier?
- Which Client Portal or Android navigation improvements would help you in the field?
- Which documentation, language or accessibility improvements would you like next?
- What would you like to see explained in a short product demonstration?

**Where to post:**
- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/liecranesupport-ai/LIE-CRANE-V2/discussions): general questions, engineering feedback and new feature ideas.
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/liecranesupport-ai/LIE-CRANE-V2/issues/new/choose): reproducible public-safe app bugs.
- **Support & Contact in the app**: trial access, purchase, billing, renewal, licensing, activation, device transfer and private customer support.

**Respect privacy:** Do not disclose working access codes, crane serial numbers, customer contact data, device/license identifiers, private logs, payment data or authentication secrets. Use invented examples or redacted screenshots.

**Important:** The product is independently developed and not endorsed by Liebherr or another equipment manufacturer. Use it only for work you are authorized to perform.
