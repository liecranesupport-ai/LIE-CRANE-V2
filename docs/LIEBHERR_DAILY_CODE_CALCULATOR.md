## The difference: Not limited to today's date

Some crane service workflows require matching the **date displayed on the crane**, rather than using the current date on the technician's phone. In LIE_CRANE V2 you can:

- Enter the crane serial number, including **leading zeros**.
- Enter **day**, **month** and **year** manually.
- Choose an **earlier date** or another date instead of relying on today's date.
- Use the **Use Today** shortcut when the current date is the right one.
- View the three labeled output levels shown by the calculator.

The manual date-selection feature is central to the app. Its usefulness and compatibility must be assessed for the specific authorized service context; it is **not a promise** that a code will work with every crane or controller.

## How the app works

1. Download and open the Android APK from the [official GitHub Release](https://github.com/liecranesupport-ai/LIE-CRANE-V2/releases/tag/LIECRANEV2).
