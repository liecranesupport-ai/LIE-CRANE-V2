Key differentiator

Questions people ask

Frequently asked questions

Can I select a date other than today?

Yes. Enter day, month and year manually, including a previous date, or use the Use Today option.

Where can I get the official Android APK?

From the LIE_CRANE V2 GitHub Release. The application is not listed on Google Play.

How can I buy a license?

Use the Client Portal's Renew / Buy License or Support & Contact section. Purchase questions are handled by the support team inside the application.

Is it officially developed by Liebherr or LICCON?

No. LIE_CRANE V2 is independent third-party software; mentions of Liebherr and LICCON identify the technical context and do not imply any manufacturer sponsorship or approval.
